What a vet can advise on.

These are the everyday concerns a vet can guide you on remotely. If it's an emergency or needs an exam, we'll tell you to go to a clinic.

Skin & coat

Itching, hairfall or a dry coat. The vet advises on coat and skin care routines.

Hairballs

The odd hairball, if your cat is still keeping food down. The vet advises on grooming and digestion.

Mouth

Bad breath while still eating. The vet advises on mouth care.

Eating less

Eating less but still taking some food. The vet advises on feeding and appetite.

General care

Bathing, grooming, and dental care for a well cat. The vet sets the routine on the call.

Food & weight

Nutrition help, or a cat getting chubby. The vet advises on diet and weight.

Before you start.

Do I need a card or account?

No. Leave your number for a vet call. No card and no account needed.

Does this replace my vet?

Never. Emergencies, bloodwork and any medical treatment belong in a clinic. We only take everyday concerns a feline vet can advise on remotely.

What if my cat is actually sick?

We'll tell you to go to a clinic. We don't collect a number for urgent or clinic-only cases.

Will a feline vet call me?

Only if your answers look like something a vet can advise on remotely. Then a feline vet usually calls within 15–30 minutes.

How long does it take?

About 2 minutes of questions. If it's a fit, you leave a number and get a vet call — usually within 15–30 minutes.

What happens on the call?

The vet reviews what you shared and gives you advice and next steps. If your cat needs a clinic, they'll say so.

Can kittens and seniors use this?

Yes, for everyday concerns and general care. Anything serious or urgent needs an in-person vet.
