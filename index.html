Before you start.

Do I need a card or account?

No. If we can treat this online, leave your number for a vet call — no card, no account. The vet prescribes supplements on that call.

Does this replace my vet?

Never. Emergencies, bloodwork, and prescription medicines belong in a clinic. We only take everyday cases a feline vet can treat online with supplements.

What if my cat is actually sick?

We'll tell you to go to a clinic. We don't collect a number or prescribe supplements for urgent or clinic-only cases.

Will a feline vet call me?

Only if your answers look like something we can treat online. Then a feline vet usually calls within 15–30 minutes and prescribes the right supplements.

How long does it take?

About 2 minutes of questions. If it's a fit, you leave a number and get a vet call — usually within 15–30 minutes.

What happens on the call?

The vet reviews what you shared and prescribes Felica supplements if they fit. If your cat still needs a clinic, they'll say so instead of selling you a plan.

Can kittens and seniors use this?

Yes — for everyday issues and prevention. Kidney, thyroid, diabetes, and emergency signs still need an in-person vet.
